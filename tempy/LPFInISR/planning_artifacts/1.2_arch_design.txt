[Implementation approach]
We will develop a modular system primarily using PyTorch for neural network implementation, NumPy and SciPy for signal processing tasks such as Fourier transforms, and Matplotlib for visualization. The approach involves loading pre-trained or self-trained super-resolution models, extracting impulse responses, decomposing the network responses into linear and non-linear parts, and calculating the FSDS metric. Key steps include synthetic impulse generation, spectral analysis, and low-pass filter simulation to test the sinc phenomenon and high-frequency injection, as well as comparison with traditional metrics.
