[Logic Analysis]
- ['main.py', 'Orchestrates the entire process: parses config, initializes DatasetLoader, Model, Trainer, optionally runs training or inference, and evaluation. Depends on the configurational setup to coordinate data loading, model instantiation, and execution flow.']
- ['dataset_loader.py', 'Defines DatasetLoader class. Responsible for loading ImageNet, applying preprocessing, running the tokenizer, and providing batch samples. Depends on tokenizer_utils.py for encoding/decoding images.']
- ['tokenizer_utils.py', 'Provides utilities for loading pre-trained tokenizers (VQ-16, KL-16). Implements encode(image) and decode(tokens). Used by DatasetLoader during data preprocessing and for translating tokens back to images during evaluation.']
- ['model.py', 'Defines TransformerModel class with causal and masked modes, plus DiffusionHead for denoising diffusion. Builds the sequence modeling architecture and the small MLP for diffusion. Depends on configuration for hyperparameters.']
- ['trainer.py', 'Implements training logic: computes diffusion loss, manages diffusion schedule, optimizes Transformer and denoising MLP parameters, handles EMA, supports class-conditional training if needed. Depends on datasets, models, and diffusion schedule.']
- ['sampling.py', 'Handles inference: performs autoregressive or masked prediction, runs reverse diffusion conditioned on autoregressive outputs. Uses the trained models to generate sequences, then decodes images via tokenizer_utils.py.']
- ['evaluation.py', 'Calculates evaluation metrics such as FID, Inception Score, and Precision/Recall on generated images. Depends on generated images and a pretrained Inception network.']
